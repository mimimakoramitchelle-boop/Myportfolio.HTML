Unseen Clues: Navigating Contaminated Evidence

In modern criminal investigations, data and physical traces serve as the silent witnesses to every scene. However, a major hurdle many investigators face is a lack of efficiency when analyzing data that has been heavily contaminated, or worse, working through cases where traditional evidential samples seem entirely absent

True forensic precision lies in seeing beyond the surface noise. When a lab sample is degraded, mixed, or compromised by environmental factors, standard analytical workflows often fall short. Bridging this gap requires advanced data reconciliation, methodical evidence tracking, and systematic analytical rigor to extract truth from chaos.
